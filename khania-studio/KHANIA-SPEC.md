## 9.1 PRODUCTION COMPLETION WINDOW & PRODUCTION CLOCK TRACKING (SOP)

To guarantee operational clarity, fairness, and transparency for both clients and Khania Studio, the following rules govern the **Production Completion Window (Komitmen Penyelesaian Produksi Awal)**:

### A. Definition of Initial Production Completion
The 24–72 working-hour commitment applies strictly to **Initial Production Delivery (Penyerahan Hasil Produksi Awal)**. This means the website has been built to the agreed scope, populated with provided material, visually designed according to reference direction, made responsive across devices, connected to core functional links, passed internal QA, and prepared for initial client review. It is NOT the final version after client-requested revisions.

### B. Project Category Windows
* **≤ 24 Working Hours Category:** Projects WITHOUT SEO article production (Starter & Bronze packages).
* **≤ 72 Working Hours Category:** Projects WITH SEO article production (Silver: 5 articles, Gold: 10 articles, max 2,500 words per article).

### C. Clock Start Condition ("READY FOR PRODUCTION")
The Production Clock starts **ONLY** when the project status is declared **READY FOR PRODUCTION**:
1. Package selected and confirmed.
2. Required business information received.
3. Text content submitted or confirmed.
4. Logo and visual assets submitted (where applicable).
5. Product/service details submitted.
6. Design reference / visual direction submitted (if applicable).
7. Contact info confirmed.
8. Khania Studio confirms that production can begin.

*Rule:* The Production Clock does **NOT** start merely because a client has made initial contact, submitted a form, engaged in consultation, paid initial fees, or submitted partial materials.

### D. Definition of Working Days & Hours
"Working hours" refer to elapsed production hours during **Working Days (Senin – Jumat)**.
* **Excluded Days:** Saturday, Sunday, and official Indonesian government public holidays shown in the official national calendar are **strictly excluded** from the production window calculation.

### E. Internal Production Clock Tracking (SOP)
Khania Studio internally logs:
1. `READY FOR PRODUCTION` timestamp
2. Production Clock start timestamp
3. Pause timestamp & reason for pause
4. Resume timestamp
5. Initial Production Delivery timestamp
6. Total counted production time

#### Tracking Logic Matrix
* **COUNTED:** Actual production time during eligible working hours (Mon-Fri).
* **NOT COUNTED:** Saturdays, Sundays, official national holidays, waiting for client materials/logo/photos, waiting for client feedback/clarifications/decisions, client-requested pauses, and client revision periods.

*Note:* This tracking system is an internal operational SOP to ensure efficiency and SLA compliance. It is not exposed as a complicated public-facing widget.

### F. Distinction: Initial Production vs. Client Revision vs. Khania Correction
* **Initial Production Delivery (`Produksi Awal`):** First complete version delivered for client review. Fulfills the 24/72-hour commitment.
* **Client Revision:** Post-delivery change requests (changes in visual preference, text changes after approval, layout/color changes, replacing approved photos, new direction). Counted against package revision allowances. Client revisions do **NOT** reset or consume the 24/72-hour commitment retroactively.
* **Khania Studio Correction:** Errors caused by Khania Studio (implementation mistakes, broken links caused by setup, missing agreed content, typos introduced by Khania, technical bugs, or layout defects). **NOT counted as client revisions** and corrected promptly by Khania Studio at no charge.

### G. Scope Change Exclusion
Requests that materially alter project scope (extra pages, extra products, extra images, or custom Web App development) operate outside the original Production Completion Window and follow additional fee rules.

## 17. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) SPECIFICATION

* **Q1: Apakah benar website bisa selesai dalam 24 jam?**  
  *A:* Ya. Untuk website tanpa artikel SEO, Khania Studio berkomitmen menyelesaikan hasil produksi awal maksimal 24 jam kerja setelah seluruh materi, informasi, dan kebutuhan website diterima lengkap serta disepakati (Ready for Production). Untuk website yang mencakup produksi artikel SEO, batas produksi awal maksimal 72 jam kerja.
* **Q2: Apa yang dimaksud 24–72 jam kerja?**  
  *A:* Perhitungan menggunakan hari kerja Senin–Jumat. Sabtu, Minggu, dan hari libur resmi pemerintah tidak dihitung sebagai waktu produksi.
* **Q3: Apakah Sabtu, Minggu dan tanggal merah dihitung?**  
  *A:* Tidak. Perhitungan produksi hanya menggunakan hari kerja. Sabtu, Minggu, dan hari libur resmi pemerintah tidak dihitung.
* **Q4: Apakah waktu menunggu materi dari klien dihitung?**  
  *A:* Tidak. Waktu yang diperlukan untuk menunggu materi, informasi, klarifikasi, persetujuan, atau respons dari klien tidak termasuk dalam waktu produksi.
* **Q5: Apakah revisi juga selesai dalam 24–72 jam?**  
  *A:* Komitmen 24–72 jam kerja berlaku untuk hasil produksi awal. Setelah website diserahkan untuk diperiksa, permintaan revisi mengikuti ketentuan revisi pada paket yang dipilih.
* **Q6: Bagaimana jika ada kesalahan dari Khania Studio?**  
  *A:* Kesalahan implementasi yang berasal dari Khania Studio bukan dianggap sebagai revisi klien. Khania Studio akan memperbaikinya sesuai kebutuhan tanpa mengurangi jatah revisi paket Anda.
